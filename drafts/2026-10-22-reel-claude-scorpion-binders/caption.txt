Anthropic's Claude designed 17 proteins that bound scorpion toxins in labs. Should AI design antivenom? Comment YES or NO.

According to Interesting Engineering, an internal Claude research model spent 64 hours generating 318,484 candidate proteins with 12 protein design tools, aimed at five scorpion neurotoxins.

Two labs, Adaptyv Bio and Twist Bioscience, tested 211 of the designs. 17 bound their target toxin, seven of them against Cn2 from a Mexican scorpion. Anthropic is releasing all 211 designs and the lab results.

This is an early step, not a treatment. The tests used lab-made toxins rather than real venom, the results sit in a technical report that has not been peer reviewed, and it is not yet known whether the proteins protect cells or animals.
