Marvell is buying Lumentum for $115 billion. Smart bet or overpaying for lasers? Comment BET or OVERPAYING.

Marvell and Lumentum announced an all-stock deal on 2 November at an implied enterprise value of $115 billion. Each Lumentum share becomes 4.5 Marvell shares, about $1,132 a share and a 23.9% premium to the 28 October close.

Marvell brings interconnect, switching and custom silicon. Lumentum brings high-performance lasers, optical transceivers and optical circuit switching. Marvell says the combined company has a $550 billion total addressable market in AI connectivity.

Marvell holders will own about 67% of the combined company. The deal is expected to close in the first half of 2028, pending stockholder and regulatory approvals. Source: Marvell's announcement.
