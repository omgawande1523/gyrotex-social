According to TechCrunch, Torsten Slok, chief economist at Apollo Global Management, says AI has delivered "zero financial payoff to corporate America outside of tech."

His note looks at the S&P 493: the S&P 500 minus Alphabet, Amazon, Apple, Meta, Microsoft, Nvidia and Tesla. Profit margins and revenue per worker for those companies look flat, and he sees no evidence of AI productivity gains.

His view on why: "AI may be a great calculator, but in business, intelligence is mostly judgment."
