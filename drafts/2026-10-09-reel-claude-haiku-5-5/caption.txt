Would you switch models for price? Comment YES or NO. Anthropic's Claude Haiku 5.5 is priced 90% below Haiku 4.5.

That cut applies to prompts up to 100,000 tokens, WinBuzzer reports. Input costs $0.10 per million tokens there, and Haiku 4.5 was $1.00.

Above 100,000 prompt tokens the rate is $0.50 per million input tokens. The context window goes to one million tokens, from 200,000 in Haiku 4.5.

Anthropic released the model on 8 Oct. It is available on the Claude Platform, Amazon Web Services, Google Cloud and Microsoft Azure. Source: winbuzzer.com.
