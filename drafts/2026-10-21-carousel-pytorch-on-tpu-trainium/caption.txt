Google and Amazon both opened their own AI chips to PyTorch apps this week, a direct move on Nvidia's lead with developers.

Google says TorchTPU, its version of PyTorch for its TPU chips, is now open source. Existing PyTorch apps can move to TPUs with a simple configuration change instead of a rewrite, TechCrunch reports.

A day earlier, Amazon announced TorchNeuron, an open source PyTorch backend for its Trainium chips, in public beta from 20 October. In eager mode, moving a model to Trainium takes one line of code, according to the PyTorch blog.

By some accounts PyTorch is used by 85% of AI apps, and most of them are written to run on Nvidia chips by default.
