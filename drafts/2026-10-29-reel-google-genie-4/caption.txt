Google DeepMind's Genie 4 holds one AI world steady for up to 2 minutes. Would you play it? Comment YES or NO.

According to TechCrunch, Google DeepMind released Genie 4 as a research preview for trusted testers. It keeps a consistent scene for up to two minutes, double its predecessor's limit, and you can type mid-simulation to change the weather, move the camera or add people and objects.

DeepMind says Genie 4 is its first real-time world model to generate audio natively: music, ambient noise and dialogue made along with the world. Everything it generates carries a SynthID watermark.

It is coming to Google AI Ultra subscribers in Project Genie and to early access partners of the Gemini API and Google AI Studio. DeepMind notes that details can still drift in longer simulations.
