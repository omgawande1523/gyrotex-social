Should more AI labs do this? Comment YES or NO. Anthropic is putting 5% of its shares into a public benefit fund.

Anthropic announced the Anthropic Public Benefit Fund on 18 October. Subject to stockholder approval, 5% of the company's shares go into it now, and the board can add 2% more for every 50% rise in the company's value above a set baseline.

The fund is a wholly owned LLC rather than a charity, so it can give in different ways, from large donations to nonprofits to direct payments to specific groups. Anthropic says none of its equity in the fund will be used for lobbying or political giving.

One area it names is AI's effect on jobs, where it has already committed $200 million. Counting earlier pledges by its founders and staff, Anthropic says roughly 20% of its shares are now set aside for the public good. Details on governance are due in the coming months, according to Anthropic.
