Would you run an 816B-parameter AI model at home? Comment YES or NO. Meta just released Muse Spark Open.

Muse Spark Open is an open-weight model from Meta Superintelligence Labs, released on 15 October. It has 816B parameters, with 42B active per token, and a context window of 1M tokens. It takes text, image, video and audio as input.

It can run on desktop hardware. According to MarkTechPost, the model runs on two Apple M5 Ultra Mac Studios with 512 GB each, linked over Thunderbolt 5.

The weights are on Hugging Face under the Muse Model License, which allows use, fine-tuning and redistribution. Hosted APIs are also available through providers such as OpenRouter and Together AI.
