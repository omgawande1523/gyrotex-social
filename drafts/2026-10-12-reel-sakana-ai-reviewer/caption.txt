Would you trust an AI peer reviewer? Comment YES or NO. Sakana AI's reviewer caught 73.43% of core-claim errors in a test.

Sakana AI calls the system Multi-Layered Review. It uses three agents built on Claude models: one reads the appendix, one checks related literature, and one writes the review, MarkTechPost reports.

To test it, the team planted 1,164 contradictions in 257 papers from five venues, including ICML and NeurIPS. On errors in a paper's core claims, the best earlier system caught 14.81%.

Each review costs about $0.47, not counting the optional literature agent. MarkTechPost also notes a limit: hidden prompt injection still sways every AI reviewer tested. Source: MarkTechPost.
